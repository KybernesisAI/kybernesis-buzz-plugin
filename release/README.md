# Kybernesis

Your own agent in Buzz, with a memory that lasts.

It runs on your Claude plan, answers in your DMs and channels, and remembers the people, decisions and details that matter across every conversation.

- Sign in with your Buzz identity. No email, no password.
- Name your agent, give it a role, and create it in about ten seconds.
- Manage your agents and see what they remember at buzz.kybernesis.ai.

Needs a Mac with Buzz and Claude turned on (Settings, Agent setup, Claude) with a Claude Pro or Max plan.
